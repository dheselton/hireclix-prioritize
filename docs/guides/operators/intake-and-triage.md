# Intake & triage

**When to use this** — New work arrived via a public form, Quick Request, or internal create-work and needs an owner.

## Steps

1. Open [Triage Inbox](/pm/inbox).
2. Open an item. Confirm client, request type, and whether it belongs under a [Live Career Site](/pm/live-sites).
3. **Claim** it, or assign someone else and set priority.
4. Choose one outcome:
   - Keep as a **request** and move it into work.
   - **Convert to Project** for SOW / multi-phase work.
   - **Decline** with a clear reason if it is out of scope.
5. Confirm the requester (and portal watchers, if any) will see status updates.

## Done when

Every overnight item is claimed, declined, or converted — nothing sits unowned.

## If this happens

- **Wrong live site parent** — edit the request/project parent or re-link from Live Career Sites.
- **Form submissions missing** — check [Forms](/pm/forms) that the form is published and the slug matches the share link (`/f/...`).
