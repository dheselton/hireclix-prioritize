# Your day (Operators)

**When to use this** — Start-of-day loop for PMs and BAs who own triage, staffing, and team health.

## Steps

1. Open [Daily Briefing](/). Scan hero chips (due today, overdue, unclaimed) and claim or assign anything urgent.
2. Go to [Triage Inbox](/pm/inbox). Claim overnight requests, set priority, link to a client or live site, or **Convert to Project** when the work is SOW-sized.
3. Check [All Work](/pm/work) in **All** mode for projects at risk; open a project to reassign or adjust dates.
4. Skim [Team Report](/pm/report) for at-risk / overdue roll-ups.
5. Log or review time on [Timesheet](/pm/time) (team toggle) if you need capacity context.

## Done when

Inbox is empty or owned, Briefing chips are actionable, and you know who owns today’s blockers.

## If this happens

- **Nothing in Inbox but Briefing shows unclaimed** — claim from Briefing or All Work; not all unclaimed items live in Inbox.
- **Submitter waiting on access** — approve them on [Team](/pm/team).
