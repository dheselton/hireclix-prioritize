# Your day (Submitter)

**When to use this** — You request work and track it; you do not run the full PM board.

## Steps

1. Sign in with your **@hireclix.com** Google account. If you see “waiting for a PM to approve,” stop here until a PM/BA activates you on Team.
2. Open [My Work](/pm/my-work) — this is your home for submitted requests, projects you’re on, and messages.
3. Submit new work via the linked public form (or [Forms](/pm/forms) / Quick Request) when you need something built or fixed.
4. Watch status and reply in portal/message threads from My Work when the team asks questions.
5. Open Help anytime from the sidebar if you need these steps again.

## What you will not see

Inbox, Team Report, Clients, Templates, Integrations, Team admin, Workload, Timeline, Timesheet, All Work board, and Snippets are hidden for submitter-only accounts.

## Done when

Your request is submitted and visible in My Work, and you know who to ping if it stalls.

## If this happens

- **Still pending after a day** — message a PM/BA to approve you on [Team](/pm/team) (they open that page; you cannot).
- **Need more screens** — ask for an additional staff role (designer, support, etc.).
