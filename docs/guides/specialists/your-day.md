# Your day (Specialists)

**When to use this** — Strategists, analysts, QA, CSMs, and support staff working assigned projects and requests.

## Steps

1. Open [Daily Briefing](/). Focus on chips for work assigned to you; claim unclaimed items in your lane when that is the team norm.
2. Use **Me** mode in the top bar to quiet noise from other teams.
3. Drive work from [All Work](/pm/work) or project/task pages; update status and leave comments when you hand off.
4. Log time on [Timesheet](/pm/time).
5. Use [Forms](/pm/forms) / public forms when you need to submit or track intake you own as requester.

## What’s different for your role

- **CSM** — you may see project-health roll-ups on [Workload](/pm/workload); use them to spot client risk early.
- **QA** — lean on project **QA / go-live** mode and task filters for QA work; cascade date changes carefully near launch.
- **Support** — live-site tickets and leftover support work matter most; coordinate with PMs on [Live Career Sites](/pm/live-sites) when you have access via project membership.
- **Strategist / Analyst** — same daily loop as above; no Snippets or Loom Library unless you also hold a production role.

## Done when

Your assignments are current, time is logged, and blockers are visible to the PM.

## If this happens

- **Need Inbox, Clients, or Team** — those are PM/BA surfaces; escalate to an operator.
- **Need Snippets** — ask for a designer/developer role if that is intentional for your seat.
