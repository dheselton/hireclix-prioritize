# Glossary

Short definitions used across Prioritize guides.

## Me mode vs All mode

Top-bar toggle. **All** shows projects and tasks across teams. **Me** narrows to work assigned to you and projects you belong to.

## Request vs project

A **request** is smaller intake work (often from a public form). A **project** is SOW-sized work, usually created from a template (e.g. Career Site). PMs can convert a request into a project from the Inbox.

## Cascade

When you change a task’s start/due (or drag the Gantt bar), dependent tasks can shift later. You confirm the list in the **Cascade Confirm** modal before it applies.

## Critical path

The longest chain of dependent tasks to go-live. Highlighted on the project Timeline (Gantt).

## Support mode

After build/QA, a career site project enters **Support mode**. It then appears under **Live Career Sites** and becomes the parent for ongoing support tickets.

## Live Career Site

A project in Support mode, listed at [Live Career Sites](/pm/live-sites). Ops health and support queues hang off these sites.

## Page groups & reserved time

Template feature that reserves capacity for unknown pages (e.g. Benefits, Locations) until Discovery. Adding real pages on the **Pages** tab consumes that reservation.

## Unclaimed

A task or inbox item with no assignee. Staff (not submitter-only) can claim it from Daily Briefing or the Inbox.

## Client portal

Token link (`/portal/...`) for external contacts to see progress, messages, and files—no Prioritize login.

## Public form

Anonymous intake at `/f/:slug` (e.g. Quick Request). Submissions land in triage for PM/BA.
