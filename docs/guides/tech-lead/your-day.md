# Your day (Tech lead)

**When to use this** — Daily loop for tech leads who run delivery ops without admin surfaces (Inbox, Team, Templates, Roadmap).

## Steps

1. Open [Daily Briefing](/). Handle due/overdue/unclaimed work you can own.
2. Use [All Work](/pm/work) and [Workload](/pm/workload) to spot bottlenecks across design/dev.
3. Check [Global Timeline](/pm/timeline) when schedule conflicts span projects.
4. Log time on [Timesheet](/pm/time).
5. Chase third-party issues on [Vendors](/pm/vendors); use [Snippets](/snippets) when reuse or incidents are involved.

## What’s different for you

You **do not** see Inbox, Team Report, Clients, Templates, Form Builder, Integrations, Team, or Roadmap. Ask a PM/BA for triage approval, roster changes, or template edits.

## Done when

Your lane’s blockers have owners, capacity looks sane, and vendor/snippet issues are tracked.

## If this happens

- **Need Inbox or Team** — that is operator-only; ping PM/BA or get a temporary PM/BA role if intentional.
