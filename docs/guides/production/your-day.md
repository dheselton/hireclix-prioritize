# Your day (Production)

**When to use this** — Designers and developers starting the day on assigned career-site work.

## Steps

1. Open [Daily Briefing](/). Work chips that are yours; claim unclaimed items in your lane if appropriate.
2. Switch the top bar to **Me** mode when you want only your assignments.
3. Open tasks from Briefing or [All Work](/pm/work) into the task workspace; update status and dates carefully (cascade may follow).
4. Log time on [Timesheet](/pm/time) as you go.
5. Use [Snippets](/snippets) / Loom Library when implementing or fixing shared patterns.

## What’s different for your role

- **Designer** — default filters lean design/content; Snippets + Loom yes; Vendors no.
- **Developer** — default filters lean dev/QA; Snippets + Loom yes; [Vendors](/pm/vendors) yes.

## Done when

Today’s assigned work is in progress or blocked with a note, and time is logged.

## If this happens

- **Need a template or client change** — ask PM/BA; those screens are operator-only.
